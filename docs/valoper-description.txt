**Why we want to validate on gno.land**

Proof of Contribution rewards building rather than bidding, and equal voting power means the set is judged on merit rather than stake. Gno plays to our strengths: years of Go and Cosmos-SDK operations transfer directly, and readable on-chain source is what we most want in infrastructure we depend on.
